Equipment Selection Guide

Active Heating vs. Active Cooling

Most proofers only heat dough. For warm ambient conditions or retarding fermentation, only thermoelectric chambers like the Sourdough Home can actively pull temperatures down.

Insulated Fabric vs. Rigid Chambers

Collapsible fabric proofers offer lightweight storage, while rigid chambers offer firmer walls, simpler sanitization, and structured internal racks.

Passive Trays & Containers

Airtight pizza dough boxes and stackable containers are designed for room-temperature bulk fermentation or cold retarding in a refrigerator without drying out dough surfaces.
