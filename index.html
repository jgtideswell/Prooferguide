Practical Buying Nuances

Heating Only vs. Peltier Cooling

Standard units only heat. In warm summer kitchens (25°C+), you cannot retard proofing without active Peltier cooling (e.g., Sourdough Home) to pull ambient temperature down.

Vessel Height & Margin

Measure your vessels including their lid or shower-cap covers. A 10-inch round banneton or 6L Cambro bucket requires a minimum of 20cm (8 inches) of internal clearance.

Footprint & Storage

Rigid units require dedicated counter or cabinet shelf space. Foldable polypropylene or fabric units collapse flat for storage in kitchen drawers between baking days.
